\paragraph{University of Washington --- Information School}

The University of Washington Information School provides Dr.\ Shah's primary research environment. The InfoSeeking Lab occupies dedicated space in Mary Gates Hall with workstations, a dedicated server for model inference and evaluation runs, and high-speed network connectivity. The iSchool provides administrative support for sponsored projects, pre-award and post-award grants management, and IRB coordination through the UW Human Subjects Division (HSD). The iSchool also provides a dedicated IRB point-of-contact who has assisted with prior Shah lab human-subjects protocols, streamlining the Aim~3 protocol submission.

\paragraph{Computing Resources}

\textit{Local and cloud inference.} The InfoSeeking Lab maintains a GPU server (4$\times$ NVIDIA A100 40\,GB) for model inference and evaluation. For large-scale evaluation runs (adversarial suite, SimBench v2 at scale), the lab uses UW Research Computing's Hyak cluster, which provides institutional allocations at no additional cost to sponsored projects. Cloud API access (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) for multi-model evaluation is budgeted in the project.

\textit{AMD hardware (pending confirmation).} AMD will provide hardware support for on-premises evaluation runs under the terms of the AMD collaboration letter; specific hardware type and quantity are pending final confirmation with AMD's Responsible AI team and will be documented in the AMD letter of collaboration included in the Single Copy Documents.

\paragraph{Human Subjects Infrastructure}

The UW Human Subjects Division provides IRB review, protocol management, and ongoing compliance support. UW HSD has extensive experience with Expedited and Exempt reviews of computer-based human-subjects studies involving de-identified data. The research team has a prior approved protocol for human-subjects evaluation studies in the InfoSeeking Lab. UW Institutional Official and Signing Official services are provided by UW's Office of Research.

\paragraph{Partner Organization Resources}

\textit{Washington State Opportunity Scholarship Foundation (WSOSF).} WSOSF will provide program staff time (estimated \textit{[N]} staff and \textit{[X]} hours per year, pending Kimberly Connors confirmation) for the Aim~1 rule curation, Aim~3 compliance reviewer study, and Year~2--3 supervised pilot. WSOSF provides de-identified or synthetic scenario materials and access to program staff for expert validation. All costs associated with WSOSF staff participation are absorbed by WSOSF as an in-kind contribution.

\textit{AMD (Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.).} AMD contributes Responsible AI advisory hours, deployment guidance for customer-hosted configurations, and hardware support (pending final confirmation). AMD's participation is documented in the AMD letter of collaboration.

\paragraph{Library and Information Resources}

UW Libraries provides access to ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, Elsevier ScienceDirect, and other scholarly databases needed for literature review and citation verification. UW Libraries staff provide research data management consultation services, including guidance on repository selection, metadata standards, and persistent identifiers.

\paragraph{Technology Transfer}

The UW CoMotion Technology Transfer Office provides patent prosecution support for the pending Sims patent, licensing administration for the VersarAI license agreement (disclosed COI), and standard technology transfer services. CoMotion services are provided at no additional cost to the project.

\paragraph{No Equipment Purchase Required}

No major equipment purchase is requested. Existing lab infrastructure, UW Research Computing allocations, AMD hardware contributions, and cloud API access are sufficient for all computational tasks in the project.
